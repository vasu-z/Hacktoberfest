# Saarthi — An AI Vision Companion

**A little more independence. A little less uncertainty.**

Imagine trying to read a medicine label, find an everyday object, or understand an unfamiliar place without being able to see it. Tasks that take seconds for some people can require extra time, effort, or assistance for others.

Saarthi aims to make these everyday moments easier for blind and low-vision people through AI-powered vision and natural voice interaction. By turning visual information into clear, spoken guidance, the goal is to help users understand their surroundings and carry out daily tasks with greater confidence and independence.

**The Problem**

Much of the world communicates through sight. Labels, signs, objects, surroundings, and everyday activities often assume that a person can simply look and understand.

For blind and low-vision people, accessing this information can mean relying on someone else, using multiple tools, or navigating uncertainty. Assistive technology should do more than describe an image — it should help people make sense of what is around them.

**Our Approach**

Saarthi is envisioned as a voice-first companion that helps users interact with the world through their phone's camera. Users should be able to ask questions, hear useful descriptions, locate everyday objects, and receive guidance in a way that feels natural and accessible.

The focus is simple: make visual information easier to access, without making interaction more complicated.

**Planned Capabilities**

- **Scene Understanding:** Describe surroundings and answer questions about what the camera sees.
- **Text Reading:** Read visible text, labels, and signs aloud.
- **Object Assistance:** Help users search for everyday objects and provide audio guidance.
- **Step-by-Step Guidance:** Assist with simple tasks and use camera input to check progress.
- **Spatial Memory:** Remember user-marked places or landmarks for future reference.
- **Voice-First Interaction:** Support voice commands and spoken feedback while working with camera input.

**Our Vision**

Saarthi is being developed around a simple belief: **access to information should not depend on the ability to see it.**

The aim is not to replace human support or promise perfect understanding of every situation. It is to build a practical assistive tool that can reduce everyday friction, support independent decisions, and help make the world a little more accessible.

Saarthi is currently a planned project, and these capabilities represent the direction we aim to build towards.
